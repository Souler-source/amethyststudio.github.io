Amethyst Studio Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 31, 2026

Amethyst Studio uses Google Sign-In to identify your account. The service stores the Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture needed to operate your account, usage limits, Premium status and processing history.

Uploaded and processed files

Files submitted for processing are stored temporarily on the configured Amethyst backend and are automatically deleted after the server retention period configured by the operator. Do not upload files you do not have permission to process.

Premium keys

Premium keys are single-use. The server stores a cryptographic hash of each key, its status, creation time, redemption time and the account that redeemed it.

Third-party services

Google Identity Services is used for sign-in. Media source platforms may receive normal network requests when a user asks Amethyst Studio to process an authorized link.

Contact

The site operator should publish a support contact before public launch.
